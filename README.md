These are tests, not client claims. Replace sample metrics, names, emails, and imagery before presenting them as a real agency website.

The depth pass adds one restrained material or spatial study per page: field rings, control-room orbits, a constellation, a route map, a system axis, a quiet horizon, or a solar diagram. Stock imagery is locally bundled under `assets/stock/` so the pages do not depend on hotlinked background images.
